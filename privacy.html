Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This policy has been drafted to align with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and its associated Rules. It has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer — please have it checked by a legal advisor before treating it as final, and fill in the bracketed placeholders below.

1. Who we are

This website (sanflix.in) is owned and operated by Kedar Consumer Products ("we," "us," "our"), the manufacturer of the Sanflix range of home cleaning products. Our registered office address is 196/25, Near Old Court, Shivaji Nagar, Narnaul, Haryana 123001. For any privacy-related queries, you can reach us at info@sanflix.in.

2. What personal data we collect

We collect personal data only when you voluntarily provide it to us, primarily through our enquiry form on the Contact page. This may include:

Your name, phone number, email address, and city. If you identify as a retailer, distributor, or bulk/institutional buyer, we may also collect your business name and estimated order volume, along with any message or details you choose to share with us.

We also use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website (see Section 6 on Cookies below).

3. Why we collect it (purpose limitation)

We collect and process your personal data solely to respond to your enquiry, share product or pricing information, process retailer/distributor/bulk partnership requests, and — where relevant — pursue a business relationship with you. We do not use your data for any purpose beyond what is stated here without asking for your fresh consent.

4. Your consent

By submitting the enquiry form on this website, you consent to us collecting and processing your personal data for the purposes described above. You may withdraw this consent at any time by emailing us at info@sanflix.in; we will stop further processing of your data on request, except where we are legally required to retain it.

5. Sharing of your data

We do not sell your personal data to any third party. To operate this website, we share limited data with trusted service providers acting on our behalf:

Formspree (our form-handling service), which delivers enquiry submissions to our email inbox. Google Analytics, which helps us understand website traffic in an aggregated, largely anonymised form. These providers are only permitted to use your data to deliver the service we've asked them to provide.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website uses Google Analytics, which places cookies on your device to help us understand how visitors use our site (for example, which pages are viewed and for how long). These cookies do not directly identify you by name. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time, though some site features may not function as intended if you do.

7. Data retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law. Enquiry data is typically retained for 1 year unless you request earlier deletion.

8. Your rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, you have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; request correction or completion of inaccurate or incomplete data; request erasure of your data (unless we are legally required to retain it); withdraw your consent at any time; and file a grievance regarding how we've handled your data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Grievance Officer below.
