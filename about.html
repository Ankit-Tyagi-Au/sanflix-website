Sanflix is the consumer-products brand of Kedar Consumer Products, focused on practical, effective and affordable household cleaning products.

Where we start

Our model starts with a strong local retail presence in one city and surrounding area, then expands through distributors, institutional/B2B partners, and eventually online/D2C and regional markets.

Who we serve

Primarily Indian households looking for effective, affordable, easy-to-use cleaning products — alongside retailers, distributors and institutional buyers.

What makes us different

Effective products, value-for-money pricing, practical packaging and reliable quality — backed by responsive support and a genuinely customer-focused approach.

Our approach

We'd rather earn trust through product demonstrations, transparent information and real customer feedback than big claims. As Sanflix grows, this page will include our story, team, and manufacturing details.
